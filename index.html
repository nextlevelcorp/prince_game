How to Rule

Advisors bring you a dilemma each season. Decide with a tap or a swipe:

◀ Swipe / tap left for the first reply.

tap right / Swipe ▶ for the second reply.

Every choice shifts your four pillars of power:

  • 👑 Authority — your grip on the crown
  • ⚔️ Army — swords that defend you
  • ❤️ People — the love of the streets
  • 💰 Treasury — the gold that buys it all

If any pillar empties or overflows, you fall. Keep all four in balance for as many years as you can — and learn what Machiavelli would have whispered in your ear.
